A NOTE FROM OUR FOUNDER

More than tests. A responsibility.

PrimePath Diagnostics began with a simple belief: that every person deserves reliable answers, compassionate care, and the confidence to make informed decisions about their health.

Behind every sample is a person, a family, a concern, and a hope. To me, laboratory diagnostics is not simply about producing numbers on a report—it is about the responsibility those numbers carry.

Our aim is to build a place where scientific precision meets humanity; where accuracy, integrity, and patient care are never just words, but principles reflected in every test we perform.

We may be starting small, but our commitment is sincere. Every sample will matter. Every report will carry responsibility. And every patient will deserve our very best.

With gratitude,
Umar Nisar
Founder, PrimePath Diagnostics
